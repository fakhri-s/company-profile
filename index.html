ABOUT THE COMPANY

About Our Company

Discover the story, vision, mission, and values behind Kapal Air Nusantara.

PT Kapal Air Nusantara

PT Kapal Air Nusantara is a company engaged in the food and beverage industry, particularly in the production and distribution of various coffee products.

Established in 2004, the company is committed to providing high-quality coffee made from selected coffee beans sourced from Indonesian plantations.

By prioritizing quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, PT Kapal Air Nusantara continues to grow into a trusted coffee company.

Vision

To become a leading coffee company in Indonesia that delivers authentic Nusantara flavors and is capable of competing in the international market.

Mission

  • To produce high-quality coffee products with the highest quality standards.
  • To develop product innovations according to consumer needs.
  • To establish sustainable partnerships with local coffee farmers.
  • To provide the best service to customers and business partners.
  • To contribute to improving the competitiveness of Indonesia's coffee industry.
